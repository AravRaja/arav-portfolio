## 1. Anchor
- Starting point: v1 grid shifted +0.5 beat → 8.37 + 0.265625 = **8.6356 s** (overlay B, confirmed by ear).
- Clap parity is taken from the 2–5 kHz crack flux (mean peak 0.656 on the clap beats vs 0.522
  on the other beats). The combined snare can't decide parity on its own because its 150–400 Hz
  body band also picks up the kick.
- Snap: median offset of the snare-flux peaks (±100 ms search at 4.8 ms hop, parabolic interp.,
  peaks ≥ 0.25) on beats 2/4, iterated → **grid anchor (beat 1) = 8.6304 s** (−5 ms vs 8.6356).
- Clap offsets vs the final grid (n = 116, post-drop): **mean +1.5 ms, median 0.0 ms, sd 28.7 ms**,
  p10..p90 −27..+37 ms, 75 % within ±30 ms.
- **Tempo drift:** per-quarter medians are +36 (8–42 s), +19, −3, −17 ms (109–142 s), a slope of −0.33 ms/s.
  The onset envelope gives the same picture on its own (−0.31 ms/s). The track is really ≈ 112.98 BPM
  (beat 0.531075 s), while the grid uses 112.94 BPM (0.53125 s). Per instructions the beat stays 0.53125 s,
  which keeps every section within about ±36 ms. `analyze_v2(beat=0.531075)` would remove the drift
  (residual sd 26 ms), but it would also move the grid away from the site's 2.125 s bar.
- **Flux timing lead (also affects v1):** a centred-STFT band flux peaks *before* the transient.
  The lead was measured with synthetic clicks and against the onset envelope: 2048-pt bands lead
  39–41 ms; the 4096-pt kick band leads ~44 ms on this song (83 ms on synthetic clicks). v2
  delays `kick` and `snare` by 40 ms (2 rows). v2 `kick` = v1 `kick` shifted 2 rows later
  (corr 0.99999999). v1's `kick` is therefore ~40 ms early. `onset` has no lead (librosa compensates it).

## 5. v1 → v2 columns
v2 table = 16 scalars + 25 lookahead = 41 columns (`song.COLUMNS_V2`, `COL_V2`, `layout(41)`).
Lookahead is still last, and the first 7 names and indices match v1.
- `beat_phase`, `bar_phase`: **v2 grid** (anchor 8.6304 s, beat 0.53125 s).
- `onset`, `energy`, `lookahead_*`: identical to v1.
- `kick`: v1 recipe delayed 40 ms.
- `after_drop`, `drop_window`: identical to v1 (8.37 s).
- new `snare`: √(150–400 Hz × 2–5 kHz percussive flux), 40 ms lead-compensated, 8 Hz low-pass, 0–1.
- new `downbeat`, `beat_pulse`, `backbeat_pulse`: gaussian pulses, σ 40 ms, on beat 1 / every beat / beats 2 and 4.
- new `eight_count_idx`: 0..31, 2-bar units from the first downbeat after the drop, restarted at each section boundary (0 before).
- new `count_in_32`: unit index within its section mod 4. Before the drop it free-runs backwards (intro = 2, 3).
- new `section_phase`: (t − unit start)/4.25 s, in [0, 1). Partial units stop early.
- new `is_breakdown`: 1 inside the four 1-bar dropouts.
- new `halftime`: 0/1 heuristic above (all 0 here).

Metronome bank: `metronome_bank(..., version=2)` (default `version=1` is byte-identical to before).
It puts the kick click on 1/3 and the snare click on 2/4 (onset = both), uses the same pulses,
runs 8-counts free from t = 0, and has no breakdowns or half-time. BPM is 90–130.
